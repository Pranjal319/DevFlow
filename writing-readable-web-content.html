Writing Readable, Accessible Web Content

Master visual hierarchy, line-height balance, WCAG AAA color contrast, and cognitive-friendly copy for web applications.

Aria Montgomery
Lead UX Researcher • Oct 15, 2025 • 4 min read

1. Measure and Line Length: The 65-Character Sweet Spot

Human eyes strain when reading lines of text that span across wide screens. The optimal reading length is between 45 and 75 characters per line (around 65 characters on average).

In CSS, enforce this effortlessly using: max-width: 65ch; on your article copy wrappers.

2. Scannable Typography and Clear Chunking

Online readers scan before they read. Use descriptive subheadings (H2, H3), bullet points, and high-contrast callouts to help readers quickly grasp key concepts.

  • Descriptive Headings: Tell the reader what they will learn rather than using generic labels.
  • Generous Line Height: Use line-height: 1.65 to 1.8 for body copy.
  • WCAG Contrast: Ensure text meets at least 4.5:1 contrast against the background surface.
